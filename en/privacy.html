Who is behind Greenstep

Greenstep is a video game published under the SEBStudios brand. For privacy, access or account deletion requests, contact greenstep.support@gmail.com. This notice covers Greenstep and these information pages; external services also have their own policies.

Access during the adults-only closed test

This version is intended for a closed test with invited participants aged 18 or over. After the startup animation and before starting Google, loading a session or connecting to our services, a vertical wheel with no preselected age asks for the user’s age. Anyone who enters an age below 18 cannot continue.

The exact age is used temporarily on the device to decide access and is not retained or sent to the server. Only an access decision —a declaration of adulthood or a block— and its policy version are stored locally. Denial persists across restarts. This is a user declaration, not identity or age verification. Clearing the app’s data may remove that decision.

The game and its services do not load while access is pending or denied. Legal documents can be read inside the app offline. This block does not itself delete accounts or progress already held on the server; deletion can be requested through the account feature or support.

Account information

The current version requires Internet access and Google sign-in. Google and Supabase provide an account identifier and may provide your email, name and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Google password. We use this information to identify your save, authenticate you and recover progress.

The session contains technical access and refresh credentials. On Android they are encrypted using AES-256-GCM and a key protected by Android Keystore, in private storage excluded from backups. While the app is running, credentials are used in memory to authenticate requests.

Progress, inventory and security

We store level progress, results, records, virtual coins, gems, skins, chests, tickets and shop operations. The canonical economic account is held on the server to synchronize installations, prevent unauthorized changes and prevent duplicate charges or rewards.

Hosting and authentication services may process IP addresses, timestamps, access logs and connection data to operate and protect the service. Browser sign-in uses a short-lived handoff and an IP hash to limit abuse. We do not use these logs for personalized advertising. There is no public chat, player messaging or item trading.

Information we do not request

We do not request contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access. We do not receive bank card details. The current version includes Google test ads and privacy-choice management, described in the dedicated advertising section of this policy.

Sound, graphics, language and control preferences are stored on the device. No full date of birth is collected. The age declaration for this test is described in the access section.

Purposes and legal grounds

We process data needed to provide the game and requested synchronization, answer support requests, protect accounts and transactions and comply with legal obligations. Where the GDPR applies, these purposes rely, as appropriate, on the requested service, legitimate interests in service security and legal obligations. Optional processing requiring consent must be explained and agreed to before it begins; Google sign-in does not replace that consent.

Test ads and privacy choices

Greenstep integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). In this adults-only closed test, interstitials use Google’s demo unit and rewarded ads use our own unit with AdMob app-level test mode enabled for the test version. These tests generate no advertising revenue. Subscriptions and real-money purchases remain disabled.

Before requesting ads, the app checks declared adult access, the online account and privacy choices through UMP. If the check or form fails, no ads are requested and the game remains playable. Only non-personalized ads are requested; this does not mean there is no personal data processing.

Google states that its advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, product and ad interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP addresses can be used to estimate approximate location. This integration does not add the game account email, account identifier, inventory or results as advertising parameters.

This version removes Android Advertising ID and ad attribution/topics permissions from the manifest, disables publisher first-party ID and removes the automatic ads initialization provider. Precise location, contacts, camera and microphone access are not requested for advertising. These measures do not eliminate other technical data Google may process when the service is used.

Ads appear only at transitions between runs, with frequency limits. Choices can be reviewed through Settings → Support and suggestions → Privacy and terms → Ad privacy options. Available choices and processing bases depend on region and Google’s message configuration; signing in does not replace consent.

Advertising technical data and privacy preferences are retained according to applicable settings and Google policies. We do not promise immediate deletion when signing out or deleting Greenstep. Please consult Google’s privacy policy and AdMob information. Before enabling live ads or changing purposes, we will update the information, choices and Play Console disclosures.

Rewarded ads are optional and add one point to the selected wheel after completing the ad and receiving Google’s signed confirmation. One rare, epic, legendary or mythic spin requires 1, 3, 5 or 8 ads respectively. Per-wheel progress and spins are saved to the account; spins can still be paid for with in-game banknotes. Prizes are random, their odds are displayed, and they are usable only in the game, without transfer or redemption for real money. To verify a reward we share a random request identifier with Google, without your email or account identifier. We receive a transaction identifier, ad unit and confirmation time to prevent duplicate rewards. These receipts are deleted with the account and cleaned up after seven days when this feature is used again; earned progress remains until account deletion.

Providers and recipients

Google provides authentication and, if purchases are enabled, Google Play will handle billing. Supabase provides authentication, database and server functions. The project's primary database is in the European eu-north-1 region. Providers may use infrastructure and subprocessors in other countries under their applicable terms and safeguards; we do not promise that all processing takes place exclusively in Europe.

Support email and its contents are processed through the email provider to answer your request. These pages are prepared for GitHub Pages, whose provider may process visitors' connection data. The pages add no first-party cookies, pixels, advertising, remote forms or visit analytics. External links are subject to the linked services' policies.

Purchases and advertising

Subscriptions are not currently available to purchase and the game does not currently display integrated ads. If purchases are enabled, Google Play will process payments. Our server may keep the product, purchase token and its hash, verification status, expiry and account link to grant and restore benefits and handle renewals, cancellations or refunds.

Before enabling live ads, other SDKs, tracking or changes affecting children's data, we must update the information and required controls. This policy is not permission to enable advertising or purchases for children.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and progress data while you maintain the service or until you request deletion, subject to legal obligations and justified security needs. Operation identities are retained with the account to prevent late retries from duplicating a charge or reward. Browser sign-in handoffs expire after five minutes, can be retrieved once, and expired records are purged by service cleanup.

When you request deletion, we verify that the request belongs to the account holder and delete associated Greenstep data. Information needed for a legal duty, fraud issue or claim is limited to what is necessary, with the reason and retention criteria explained when relevant. Provider backups follow their own cycles and access controls. Signing out does not delete an account, and uninstalling does not automatically erase a cloud save.

To confirm retries after an interruption, a cryptographic request fingerprint is retained for seven days, without an email, player identifier or readable token. If the sign-in identity is shared with another game, the identity needed for that game and a deletion timestamp are retained to stop earlier sessions from restoring deleted Greenstep progress.

Children and families

Content ratings, the age for data consent and the capacity to make purchases are separate questions and vary by country. Where parental consent is legally required for processing, a child must not independently create or use features requiring it before the necessary safeguards are in place. In Spain the threshold for independently consenting to consent-based processing is 14; this is not a universal minimum age to play.

The current version does not have a verified parental-consent system or its own supervised child accounts. A Google account or a declared age alone does not prove parental permission. Parents and legal guardians can contact us to review, restrict or delete data if safeguards appear to be missing. See the Families page for more information.

Planned parental authorization: KWS (not yet active)

We are evaluating Kids Web Services (KWS), provided by Kids Web Services Ltd, an Epic Games group entity, for adult verification and parental consent management. KWS is not yet connected to Greenstep: the current app does not send data to this provider. This section describes a planned integration and does not enable child access.

If enabled, the proposed verification flow uses the parent or guardian’s email address and language, and the child’s country or region. Consent management may also require a request identifier and the permissions requested. The purpose will be to verify the adult, obtain their decision about specific features and manage withdrawal, using only necessary data.

Depending on the verification method available, the adult may need to provide additional information directly within KWS or its providers’ environment. Greenstep should not receive identity documents, verification images or full card details. Sending game inventory, balances or match results to KWS is not planned for adult verification.

Before enabling the service, we will update this policy and show the adult a notice specifying the actual data and purposes, the controller for each processing activity, legal bases, retention, possible international transfers and how to exercise rights or withdraw authorization. We will also link to the KWS privacy policy. Verifying that someone is an adult does not itself obtain consent to process a child’s data.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, and withdraw consent for future processing. Contact support, preferably from the email linked to your account. We will request only reasonable, necessary verification. Do not send passwords, Google codes or identity documents unless specifically and appropriately requested.

We will respond within the applicable legal deadlines. You may contact your competent data protection authority; in Spain this is the AEPD. The Account deletion page explains how to submit a request without having the app installed.

Policy changes

Updates will be published here with their date. If a change needs new information or consent, we will provide it before that processing begins. Describing a future feature here does not mean it has been enabled.
