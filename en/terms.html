The service

Greenstep is a casual game from SEBStudios. These terms describe use of the game and support. If you lack legal capacity to accept them, your legal representative must be involved. The current version requires Internet access and a Google account to identify and synchronize progress.

Access during the adults-only closed test

This version is intended for a closed test with invited participants aged 18 or over. After the startup animation and before starting Google, loading a session or connecting to our services, a blank input asks for the user’s age. Anyone who enters an age below 18 cannot continue.

The exact age is used temporarily on the device to decide access and is not retained or sent to the server. Only an access decision —a declaration of adulthood or a block— and its policy version are stored locally. Denial persists across restarts. This is a user declaration, not identity or age verification. Clearing the app’s data may remove that decision.

The game and its services do not load while access is pending or denied. Legal documents can be read inside the app offline. This block does not itself delete accounts or progress already held on the server; deletion can be requested through the account feature or support.

Your account and progress

Use an account you are authorized to use and protect its access. Do not share passwords or verification codes. The server holds the canonical inventory and balance; editing local files does not grant ownership of account items. Saves synchronize automatically when the service is accessible. A genuine save conflict may require choosing which save to keep.

After uninstalling or moving to another device, sign in with the same account to recover synchronized progress. Connection failures can leave changes pending. Signing out stops use of that session; it does not erase server data.

Virtual currency, chests, wheels and skins

Current coins and gems are earned through play and are not offered for real money. Chests and wheels grant virtual rewards according to the tables shown in the game. Items and balances cannot be transferred between players, sold or redeemed for money. They are not a payment method or financial product.

Costs and probabilities can be checked before opening a chest or spinning. Account rewards are calculated on the server. We do not promise a particular skin on any individual draw. Fixing errors, balance issues or abuse may require justified adjustments; statutory consumer rights are not excluded.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions cannot currently be purchased. If an ad-removal subscription is enabled, the Google Play purchase screen must show its price, period, renewal terms and benefit before confirmation. Visiting these pages or signing in does not make a purchase.

An auto-renewing subscription follows its terms until cancelled. It will be managed and cancelled in Google Play; uninstalling the game or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel it. Refunds follow Google Play procedures and applicable legal rights. Check any active subscription before requesting account deletion.

Responsible use

Do not access others' accounts, exploit vulnerabilities, submit false operations or interfere with the service. Access may be restricted when necessary to protect users or investigate abuse. A synchronization discrepancy is not automatically evidence of cheating; support can review it.

The game currently has no player chat, item trading, cash prizes or active multiplayer. Substantial changes to these features require corresponding information and safeguards.

Children, availability and legal rights

If you are a minor, read the Families information with a responsible adult. Legal restrictions on data and purchases apply even if the content rating considers the game appropriate for your age. An optional subscription alone does not make a game a 13+ or 18+ product.

The service may receive maintenance, updates and fixes, and interruptions may occur. We will aim to resolve issues reported to support. These terms do not exclude mandatory legal rights or guarantees or prevent you from contacting competent authorities.
