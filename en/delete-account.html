Delete from the game

Open Settings → Delete account. Read the warning about permanently losing skins, coins, gems and all progress, tick the acknowledgement box, then tap “Delete permanently”. The game confirms completion after server deletion and clears this save from the device. If the connection is interrupted, you can retry the same request without duplicating the operation.

How to request deletion

Email greenstep.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Greenstep account”. Preferably send from the Google email used to sign in. Say that you want your Greenstep account and associated data deleted. Include your player ID if available; it is not required to start a request.

The button opens a draft in your email application. Your request is sent only when you send that email. If no email app is configured, copy the address and use your usual email service. This is a manual request, not an automatic deletion confirmation.

Verification and data removed

We verify that the request belongs to the account before irreversible deletion. If you write from another email or act as a legal representative, we will explain the minimum necessary verification. Do not send passwords, verification codes, bank cards or identity documents on your own initiative.

The request covers Greenstep progress, levels and records, virtual balance, skins, chests, tickets, operations and associated account data. You will lose access to this inventory and save restoration. If you need a data copy first, mention it in your request.

What this request does not delete

It does not delete your Google account or other services' data. If a sign-in identity is legitimately used by another game from the publisher, a request limited to Greenstep should not delete that game's progress. We will review separation of data before proceeding.

It does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Purchases are currently unavailable in Greenstep, but if you have a future active subscription, cancel it in Google Play to stop renewals.

Timing and possible exceptions

We will reply within applicable legal deadlines, explain the next steps and confirm completion. Limited information may need to be retained for a legal duty, claim or fraud issue; where relevant we will explain its categories, reason and retention criteria. Provider backups follow their own deletion cycles and access controls.

Uninstalling and signing out do not replace a deletion request. Parents and legal guardians can use the same support channel.

To confirm retries after an interruption, a cryptographic request fingerprint is retained for seven days, without an email, player identifier or readable token. If the sign-in identity is shared with another game, the identity needed for that game and a deletion timestamp are retained to stop earlier sessions from restoring deleted Greenstep progress.
