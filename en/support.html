Help and suggestions

Email greenstep.support@gmail.com. For an issue, include the game version, device model, what you were doing and the error message. Screenshots may help, but hide personal information that is not needed.

Do not publish your email, account ID or receipts in a public GitHub repository. We do not need your password, Google codes or private keys to help you.

Privacy, families and deletion

Use the same email to exercise privacy rights, ask about children or request account deletion. For deletion, use the subject “Delete Greenstep account” and follow the dedicated page. A support message does not automatically delete an account; requests are verified first.

Purchases and security

Purchases are not currently enabled. If they become available and an issue occurs, identify the product and issue without sending complete bank details. To report a security issue, use the subject “Greenstep security” and explain reproduction steps without including other people's credentials.

Contact us

greenstep.support@gmail.com

Do not send passwords or verification codes.
