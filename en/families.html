Age and content ratings are different

A content rating describes whether the game's themes are appropriate. It is not consent to data processing or capacity to buy a subscription. The official Google Play rating will come from its questionnaire; we do not announce a PEGI rating that has not been assigned.

Access during the adults-only closed test

This version is intended for a closed test with invited participants aged 18 or over. After the startup animation and before starting Google, loading a session or connecting to our services, a blank input asks for the user’s age. Anyone who enters an age below 18 cannot continue.

The exact age is used temporarily on the device to decide access and is not retained or sent to the server. Only an access decision —a declaration of adulthood or a block— and its policy version are stored locally. Denial persists across restarts. This is a user declaration, not identity or age verification. Clearing the app’s data may remove that decision.

The game and its services do not load while access is pending or denied. Legal documents can be read inside the app offline. This block does not itself delete accounts or progress already held on the server; deletion can be requested through the account feature or support.

How the current version works

Greenstep requires Google sign-in and Internet access to synchronize its account. It has no public chat, player messaging, item transfers or active multiplayer. It currently contains no advertising SDK and subscriptions cannot be purchased.

A dedicated child-account system and verified parental-consent process have not yet been enabled. Google sign-in, an age declaration or a checkbox alone do not demonstrate permission from a responsible adult. When legally required, valid permission must be obtained before the relevant processing. Questions about children's access should be directed to support before creating or using an account requiring these safeguards.

Virtual items and purchases

Chests and the reward wheel use virtual coins earned through play; real-money coin or gem purchases are not offered in this version. Rewards cannot be sold, transferred or converted to money. Probabilities are available in the game.

If advertising or purchases are introduced, the audience's required safeguards must be applied. For children and unknown-age users, hiding an individual advert is not enough: SDKs, data handling and commercial controls must also be appropriate. Publishing this page neither enables those features nor certifies compliance with Families policies.

How a responsible adult can help

Review the Google Play listing and rating, app permissions, account being used and device parental controls. Accompany early play and agree on breaks. Do not share passwords or codes.

To review, correct or delete a child's data, email greenstep.support@gmail.com and explain that you are the parent or legal guardian. We will ask only for reasonable verification needed to handle the request. See the Privacy policy and Account deletion pages too.
